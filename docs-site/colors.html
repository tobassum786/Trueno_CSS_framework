Color Plates

Every hue in Trueno CSS is a color plate — a tonal scale of steps defined once in src/abstract/_colors.scss and compiled into the CSS custom properties below. Hover a swatch to reveal its token, click to copy it, and watch the preview update live. Source: docs/colors.md
